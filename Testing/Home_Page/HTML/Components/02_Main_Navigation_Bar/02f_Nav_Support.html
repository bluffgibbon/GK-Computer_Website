    • Troubleshooting ▸

      Common Issues & Initial Fixes

      Before scheduling a service call, try these quick checks:

      • Restart your computer or device — this solves 60% of common issues
      • Check all cables, power cords, and external devices
      • Verify your WiFi/network connection and router lights
      • Update your system (Windows Update, macOS Software Update)
      • Scan for malware using your installed antivirus tools
      • Try disconnecting peripherals one at a time
      • If none of the above works, on-site support may be required
