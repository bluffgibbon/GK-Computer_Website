    IT Consulting, Planning & Technology Guidance

    Practical IT guidance for home users, small businesses, and independent professionals who need clear technical direction without unnecessary complexity. I help identify what is actually needed, what is wasting money, and what changes will improve reliability, performance, and workflow. This service is built for real-world decision-making around systems, upgrades, networking, software, website functionality, and day-to-day technology use.

    Hardware & Upgrade Planning

    Many technology problems start with buying the wrong hardware or upgrading the wrong part. I help plan around actual needs so the result is useful, compatible, and worth the money spent.

    • Desktop, laptop, and workstation buying guidance
    • Upgrade planning for RAM, storage, cooling, and graphics
    • Compatibility review before purchasing parts or peripherals
    • Budget-focused recommendations based on real workload needs
    • Avoiding overspending on unnecessary hardware

    Home Office & Small Business IT Direction

    Small environments often grow without a real plan, which leads to messy setups, wasted time, and avoidable problems. I help clean up that direction and turn it into a workable plan.

    • Planning for small office computers, printers, and shared devices
    • Basic network and connectivity improvement recommendations
    • Workflow review for how devices are used day to day
    • Recommendations for more reliable and manageable setups
    • General technology organization for small business environments

    Software, Services & Platform Guidance

    Choosing software and services without a plan often creates unnecessary cost or technical problems later. I help evaluate what tools make sense and how they should fit together.

    • Advice on operating system, application, and productivity tool choices
    • Recommendations for backups, storage, and routine maintenance tools
    • Planning around website, email, and business software needs
    • General guidance on cloud-connected and local software workflows
    • Helping simplify tool overload and overlapping services

    Project & Technical Decision Support

    Sometimes the issue is not a broken machine but a technical decision that needs to be made correctly the first time. I provide input on practical implementation choices before money or time gets wasted.

    • Consulting before website, workstation, or network changes
    • Review of technical options for new business or home setups
    • Advice on phased upgrades versus full replacement
    • General troubleshooting strategy for recurring technical problems
    • Translating technical choices into clear next steps
