    AI can be helpful, but it can also be confidently wrong or expose information carelessly if used without judgment. I teach practical habits for using it responsibly.

    • Checking AI output for accuracy before relying on it

    • Recognizing hallucinations, weak answers, and misleading results
    • Building safer workflows around review and verification
    • Using AI as a tool rather than a replacement for judgment
