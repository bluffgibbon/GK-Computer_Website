This service is built around real-world small network support — not large enterprise infrastructure.     Wi-Fi Coverage & Stability

    Wi-Fi problems are one of the most common issues in homes and small offices. Weak signal, dropped connections, poor placement, and bad router settings waste time and interrupt work.

    • Weak Wi-Fi signal areas
    • Random disconnects and poor router placement
    • Device connection problems
    • Congestion from too many connected devices
    • Basic coverage issues in small spaces

    Wired Network Support

    For systems that need a more stable connection than Wi-Fi, wired networking is often the better choice. I help set up and troubleshoot wired connections for desktops, printers, and office equipment.

    • Ethernet connection troubleshooting
    • Small switch setup and port testing
    • Desktop-to-network connection issues
    • Printer and device access over the local network

    Troubleshooting & Reset Services

    Sometimes the network does not need to be replaced — just corrected. I diagnose common small-network problems and rebuild the setup into something cleaner and more reliable.

    • Router reset and reconfiguration
    • Reconnecting devices after ISP or hardware changes
    • Fixing misconfigured network settings
    • Resolving device access and internet issues
    • Cleaning up old or unnecessary settings

    Networking Service Levels

    Level 1 — Basic Network Setup ($)

    Best for simple home or small office networking needs. Includes router setup, Wi-Fi configuration, connecting devices, basic printer connectivity, and getting the network online and working properly. Pricing varies based on the number of devices and the condition of the current setup.

    Level 2 — Small Office Network Setup & Device Integration ($$)

    Best for small offices that need more than basic internet access. Can include router and switch setup, wired and wireless device configuration, printer setup, guest Wi-Fi configuration, workstation connection, and general small office network organization. Pricing varies based on network size, number of devices, and setup complexity.

    Level 3 — Network Troubleshooting & Reconfiguration ($$$)

    Best for unstable, misconfigured, or expanded small networks that need deeper troubleshooting and cleanup. Can include resolving disconnects, Wi-Fi instability, IP conflicts, device communication issues, router resets and reconfiguration, reconnecting office devices after equipment changes, and improving overall network reliability. Pricing varies based on troubleshooting time, network layout, and correction needed.
