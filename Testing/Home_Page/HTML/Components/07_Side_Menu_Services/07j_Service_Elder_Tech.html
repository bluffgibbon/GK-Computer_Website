    Elder Tech Support, Device Help & Technology Education

    Patient, one-on-one support for older adults who want help using technology with less confusion and less frustration. This service is built around clear explanations, hands-on setup, and step-by-step guidance for the devices and tasks that matter in daily life. Whether the goal is learning how to use a computer, understanding a smartphone, staying in touch with family, or avoiding common online scams, I focus on making technology understandable and usable at a comfortable pace.

    Computer & Device Basics

    A lot of problems come from missing the basics early on. I help build comfort with the everyday parts of using a computer, tablet, or phone so the device feels less intimidating.

    • Basic desktop, laptop, tablet, and phone use
    • Understanding clicks, taps, windows, files, and folders
    • Connecting to Wi-Fi and basic internet use
    • Learning device settings, volume, brightness, and common controls
    • Help with keyboards, touchpads, mice, and accessibility basics

    Email, Messaging & Staying Connected

    For many people, the main goal is simple: stay connected and communicate without stress. I help set up and explain the tools that make that possible.

    • Email account setup and understanding inbox basics
    • Sending messages, attachments, and photos
    • Video calling and communication app setup
    • Basic contact list organization and call help
    • Learning how to recognize legitimate messages versus suspicious ones

    Online Safety & Scam Awareness

    One of the most important parts of technology education is learning what not to trust. I explain common scam patterns and safer habits in plain language without overwhelming technical detail.

    • Recognizing phishing emails, fake pop-ups, and scam calls
    • Safer password habits and account protection basics
    • How to avoid unsafe downloads and misleading websites
    • Checking links, attachments, and online requests more safely
    • What to do if something suspicious already happened

    Everyday Technology Tasks

    Technology is easier to learn when it is tied to real tasks. I help with common day-to-day activities so customers leave with something useful they can repeat on their own.

    • Printing documents and basic printer use
    • Opening, saving, and finding files again later
    • Using web browsers for bills, appointments, and account access
    • Photo transfer and basic file sharing help
    • Basic app installation and update help on supported devices

    Elder Tech Support Service Levels

    Level 1 — Basic Device Help & Setup ($)

    Best for simple setup, basic questions, and getting more comfortable with a computer, phone, or tablet. Can include Wi-Fi setup, email help, basic device use, app access, and step-by-step answers to common questions. Pricing varies based on session length and number of devices.

    Level 2 — Guided Learning & Everyday Use Support ($$)

    Best for customers who want more than a quick fix and need patient guidance using technology in daily life. Can include repeated instruction, communication app help, printing, file use, basic online tasks, and practical learning sessions built around real needs. Pricing varies based on lesson depth and follow-up support.

    Level 3 — Ongoing Support, Safety & Confidence Building ($$$)

    Best for customers or families who want broader support across devices, recurring help sessions, and stronger online safety habits. Can include continued one-on-one education, account help, scam awareness coaching, device organization, and a more complete support approach designed to build confidence over time. Pricing varies based on frequency, device count, and overall support scope.
