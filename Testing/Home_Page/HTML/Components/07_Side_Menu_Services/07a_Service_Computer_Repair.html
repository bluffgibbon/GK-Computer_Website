    Desktop Computer Repair & Cleaning

    Reliable, secure, and performance-focused solutions for desktop systems and custom-built PCs. I diagnose the problem correctly and fix it at the source — Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, and custom Intel/AMD builds.

    Note: Apple products are not serviced for physical disassembly or deep cleaning due to manufacturer restrictions.

    Desktop Repair Solutions

    I handle a wide range of desktop hardware and software issues. Every repair follows a structured diagnostic process to identify the root cause, not just the symptom.

    • Slow performance and system lag
    • System crashes, freezing, or boot failures
    • Blue screen errors and OS corruption
    • Overheating from dust buildup or poor airflow
    • Failing hard drives and storage issues
    • Power supply failures and startup issues
    • GPU and RAM-related performance problems
    • Software errors and corrupted installations

    Desktop Internal Cleaning

    Desktop systems are highly prone to dust buildup, especially in high-airflow cases. My cleaning service includes:

    • Full internal dust removal (case, fans, heatsinks, PSU, GPU)
    • Airflow inspection and cooling performance check
    • Cable path inspection for proper airflow
    • Optional thermal paste replacement (CPU/GPU)
    • Fan operation and noise check

    Cleaning Service Levels

    Level 1 — Basic Cleaning ($)

    Case opened and cleaned without removing internal components. Dust removed from accessible areas. Best for light to moderate buildup. Pricing varies by system size and condition.

    Level 2 — Intermediate Cleaning ($$)

    Partial disassembly for deeper cleaning. GPU and panels removed to reach restricted areas. Fans, heatsinks, and airflow paths cleaned thoroughly. Pricing varies by complexity.

    Level 3 — Full Deep Clean ($$$)

    Complete teardown and rebuild. All components removed, fully cleaned, and reinstalled. Includes thermal paste replacement. Pricing varies by build complexity and condition.
