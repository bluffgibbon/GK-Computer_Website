    Smart Phone, Tablet & Mobile Device Support

    Practical help for phones and tablets that are slow, confusing, misconfigured, or no longer doing what they should. I help with setup, troubleshooting, app issues, account problems, storage cleanup, and day-to-day device use so the device becomes reliable and easier to manage. This service is built around real mobile support for personal, family, and small business use rather than carrier sales or scripted support responses.

    Device Setup & Basic Configuration

    New phones and tablets often need more setup than expected. I help get the device configured correctly so it is ready for real use from the start.

    • Initial setup for smartphones and tablets
    • Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and basic connectivity configuration
    • Email, app store, and account sign-in setup
    • Basic privacy, notification, and device setting adjustments
    • Home screen cleanup and general usability setup

    App, Account & Sync Issues

    A lot of phone and tablet problems come from accounts not syncing properly, apps failing to behave, or settings not being tied together correctly. I help straighten those issues out so the device works as expected.

    • App troubleshooting and cleanup of misbehaving applications
    • Email and account sync issue correction
    • Calendar, contact, and photo sync troubleshooting
    • Password, sign-in, and account access guidance
    • Fixing notification, permission, and background activity issues

    Performance, Storage & Everyday Troubleshooting

    Mobile devices slow down over time, fill up with unused apps and files, or start behaving unpredictably. I help clean up the device and correct the common causes of poor performance.

    • Storage cleanup and identifying what is filling device space
    • Slow performance and freezing issue troubleshooting
    • Basic update support for apps and device software
    • Battery drain and background usage review
    • General troubleshooting for calls, messaging, and connection problems

    Photo, File & Device Transition Help

    Moving from one device to another or keeping photos and files organized can be one of the most frustrating parts of mobile device ownership. I help make that process more manageable and less risky.

    • Photo and video transfer between devices and computers
    • Basic file organization and cleanup on mobile devices
    • Helping move to a replacement phone or tablet
    • Checking backup status before device changes or resets
    • Guidance for safer transition to new hardware

    Smart Phone & Tablet Support Service Levels

    Level 1 — Basic Mobile Device Help ($)

    Best for simple setup, app help, Wi-Fi issues, account sign-in problems, and general device questions. Can include configuration, light troubleshooting, and day-to-day support for phones and tablets. Pricing varies based on device type and session length.

    Level 2 — Device Cleanup & Account Troubleshooting ($$)

    Best for devices with performance problems, sync issues, storage problems, or broader configuration trouble. Can include app cleanup, account correction, photo or file help, battery and settings review, and more involved troubleshooting. Pricing varies based on device condition and issue depth.

    Level 3 — Advanced Device Transition & Recovery Support ($$$)

    Best for more involved mobile support situations such as moving to a new device, resolving multi-account confusion, recovering day-to-day usability after major setup problems, or preparing a device for reset or replacement. Pricing varies based on time, transfer needs, and overall complexity.
