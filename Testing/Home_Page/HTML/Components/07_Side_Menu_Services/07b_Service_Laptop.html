    Laptop Repair & Cleaning

    Reliable, secure, and performance-focused solutions for laptops and portable systems. I provide professional laptop repair and cleaning services designed to restore performance, eliminate issues, and extend the lifespan of your device.     Laptop Repair Solutions

    I handle a wide range of laptop hardware and software issues. Every repair follows a structured diagnostic process to identify the root cause, not just the symptom.

    • Slow performance and system lag
    • System crashes, freezing, or boot failures
    • Overheating and thermal issues
    • Battery not charging or rapid battery drain
    • Broken or failing charging ports
    • Screen issues (cracked, flickering, no display)
    • Keyboard and trackpad problems
    • Failing storage drives and data issues
    • Software errors and corrupted operating systems

    Laptop Internal Cleaning

    Laptops are compact and prone to heat buildup, making cleaning critical for performance and longevity. My cleaning service includes:

    • Internal dust removal (fans, vents, heatsinks)
    • Cooling system inspection and airflow check
    • Thermal paste replacement (when accessible)
    • Fan performance and noise check

    Pricing

    Cleaning cost depends on the laptop model and how much disassembly is needed — anywhere from a simple external clean to a full teardown and reassembly. I'll give you a clear price before I start.
